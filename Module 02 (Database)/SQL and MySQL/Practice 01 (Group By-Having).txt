

# GROUP BY & HAVING – Practice Problem Statements

---

## 🟢 EASY LEVEL

1. Count the number of orders placed by each user.

---

2. Find the total quantity sold for each product.

---

3. Display each order ID along with the total number of products in that order.

*(Hint: same order can have multiple items)*

---

## 🟡 INTERMEDIATE LEVEL

4. Find users who have placed more than 1 order.

---

5. Find products that have been ordered more than once (total quantity > 1).

---

6. Find orders that contain more than one distinct product.

*(Hint: `COUNT(product_id)`)*

---

## 🔴 TRICKY LEVEL

7. Find users who have placed exactly one order.

---

8. Find products that were ordered by more than one different user.

*(Hint: `DISTINCT` + grouping)*

---

9. Find users whose total order value exceeds 2000.

*(Hint: `SUM(price × qty)` across multiple tables)*

---

🔟 Find orders where the total order amount is greater than the average order amount.

*(Hint: GROUP BY + HAVING + subquery)*

---

## 🧠 How to Approach Each Problem

For every problem, ask yourself:

1. Which table(s) hold the raw data?
2. What defines a “group”?
3. What aggregate function is needed?
4. Is the filter row-level (`WHERE`) or group-level (`HAVING`)?

---

## 🎯 Practice Strategy (Highly Recommended)

* Solve 1–3 → confidence
* Solve 4–6 → correctness
* Solve 7–10 → mastery
